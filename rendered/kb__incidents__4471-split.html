parser. The booking was made for one date and the invite or reminder shows the same digits with day and month swapped: 8 July turns into 7 August. It comes in by app, by email and through booking-v2, from every day-first tenant. This is the bug #4471 describes.

hint. The same swap, but only through the booking-v3 form at day-first tenants. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type month first, and the form then reads their input the tenant’s way. A parser that reads day first does not help: the input is already month first. The fix is in the form.

one-day-move. The date moves by one day, not day ↔ month: “Tuesday 12 May” shows as “Monday 11 May”, a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and a client in Toronto gets Monday, an invite arrives before the booking was made, every June booking is a day off. That is time zones, not date order. The desk noted these reports and then counted them under parser by tenant; a parser fix leaves every one of them where it is.
